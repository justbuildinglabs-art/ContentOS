# ContentOS 0.6.0: decisions made during implementation

Every ruling the controlling session made on Leslie's behalf while building 0.6.0 (plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-23-successful-discovery.md`, 2026-09-23 and 2026-09-24), in order, then Leslie's own calls from the live check. Each ruling ends with what it costs if it was wrong. Revisit any of them by editing the code or the spec; nothing here is load-bearing beyond what the tests pin.

## Before Task 1

## Found in the live check, not fixed in 0.6.0

- The Apify account is on a $5 monthly limit ($3.81 used in the cycle ending 2026-10-17). Apify lowers each run's `maxTotalChargeUsd` to the credit left, so a run near the limit can come back short without being marked partial. A pre-flight check of `/users/me/limits` for research and discover would catch it.
- The 540-second budget left the reels step 146 and 140 seconds in the two runs, because the account checks take 3 to 3.5 minutes each. The panel runs in the background, outside the 10-minute Bash limit, so it could take a longer budget.
- A panel that closes on the idle timeout can't show the finished run again: a new panel starts empty. Picking from `discovery.json` needs the chat steps or another paid run.
