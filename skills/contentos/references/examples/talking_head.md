## Hook

**Primary (approach: pain call-out)**
Spoken: You've started the same morning routine four times this year. It's March.
On-screen text: Attempt four.

**Backup (approach: contrarian)**
Spoken: Streaks are the reason you quit. The counter hits zero and you never open the app again.
On-screen text: Zero is a quit message.

## Demo moment

The missed day tap. Thumb hits Tuesday, one leaf falls, and the day count stays on nine. Hold the shot two
full seconds so the number is readable when someone pauses. From Demo moments in `product.md`.

## CTA

**Primary (direct ask)**
You've deleted five habit apps. This one holds one habit. Free. Start tonight.

**Backup (open loop)**
We deleted the streak counter on purpose. The reason is in the app.

## Caption

One habit. No counter. Miss a day and you lose a leaf, not nine days of work.
The first week feels too slow. That's deliberate.

## Production notes

- Audio: one voice, phone mic is fine. No music under the first three seconds, quiet bed from 0:09.
- Shots: four setups, all handheld. Shot one is the phone only, no face. Shots two to four are over the
  shoulder on the screen, with the face back in frame for the CTA.
- Text style: four beat cards plus the hook card and the CTA card, six words or fewer each, bottom
  third, high contrast, readable with sound off.
- Read time: about 32 seconds at 2.5 words per second, counting the primary hook and the primary CTA.
- Word count: 54 spoken words plus 20 on-screen words in the Beats table, 74 against a budget of 75.
  Bracketed markers count as zero.
- Beat four needs the founder's retention number before filming. If it never arrives, cut beat four and
  end on the leaf.

## What changed vs source

Kept: the pain call-out mechanism, the cold open on a phone screen with no face, and the four beat shape.
The source also put its proof number in the final two seconds.

Changed, roughly 15 percent: the source blamed the viewer's discipline, this one blames the counter. The
source used a gym habit, this one uses a morning routine because that is where Sprout's audience quits.
Every spoken line is new. The source's on-screen text ran in the center of frame, ours sits in the bottom
third so the product card stays visible.
