## Hook

**Primary (approach: pain call-out)**
Spoken: You spent this morning cleaning up code you didn't write. Again.
On-screen text: Cleanup, day three.

**Backup (approach: contrarian)**
Spoken: The agent isn't the problem. You handed it the whole repo and walked off to make coffee.
On-screen text: You gave it too much.

## Production notes

- Audio: one voice, phone mic is fine. No music under the first three seconds, quiet bed from 0:10.
- Shots: four setups, all handheld. Shots one and four are to camera. Shots two and three are top-down on
  the notebook, so the handwriting is the only thing moving.
- Text style: four beat cards plus the hook card and the CTA card, six words or fewer each, bottom third,
  high contrast, readable with sound off.
- Read time: about 32 seconds at 2.5 words per second, counting the primary hook and the primary CTA.
- Word count: 56 spoken words plus 17 on-screen words in the Beats table, 73 against a budget of 75.
  Bracketed markers count as zero.
- Beat four needs the real cleanup number before filming. If it never arrives, cut beat four and end on
  the pen tap.

## What changed vs source

Kept: the pain call-out mechanism, the cold open with no face and no greeting, and the four beat shape.
The source also held its proof number to the final two seconds.

Changed, roughly 15 percent: the source blamed the viewer for trusting the tool, this one blames the
instruction they gave it. The source used a design handoff, this one uses a test, because that is where
this audience actually loses the morning. Every spoken line is new. The source ran its text across the
middle of frame, ours sits in the bottom third so the notebook stays visible.
