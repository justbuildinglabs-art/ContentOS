## Demo moment

The greyed out Add button. Record the tap that does nothing, then the tooltip with the day count. Follow it
with the missed day tap so the viewer sees both halves of the rule in one take. From Demo moments in
`product.md`.

## CTA

**Primary (direct ask)**
You've quit five of these. Sprout holds one habit and nothing else. Free to start.

**Backup (open loop)**
The Add button stays grey for 14 days. There's a reason, and it's in the app.

## Caption

Setup is one field. Then the app locks you out of starting anything else for two weeks.
It's annoying on day two. That is the entire product.

## Production notes

- Audio: voiceover recorded after the screen capture. Keep the real tap and keyboard sounds in the mix,
  low. No music under the first three seconds.
- Shots: one continuous screen recording at 60fps, cut into five segments. The rival grid in segment one
  is a stand-in build, not a competitor's app, so nothing identifiable ships.
- Text style: burned-in labels, five words or fewer, anchored top-left so they never cover the Add button.
- Read time: about 41 seconds at 2.5 words per second, counting the primary hook and the primary CTA.
- Word count: 74 spoken words plus 22 on-screen words in the Beats table, 96 against a budget of 100.
  Bracketed markers count as zero.
- Beat five holds a placeholder. Without the real retention number, cut it and end on the missed day.

## What changed vs source

Kept: the bold claim mechanism, the uninterrupted screen recording, and the habit of naming the tap a beat
before it happens. The source also opened on a competitor-style screen before showing its own.

Changed, roughly 20 percent: the source demoed an export feature, ours demos a restriction, which is the
only thing in Sprout a competitor cannot copy in a week. The source's proof was a review screenshot, and we
cannot use one because no testimonial is listed under Proof assets, so beat five carries a placeholder
instead. All wording is ours.
