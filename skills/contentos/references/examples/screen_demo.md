## Payoff

The Friday split screen. The same nine lines sit in the editor on the left and in the free build log email
on the right, word for word, with the send button in shot. Hold it three seconds so a paused frame shows
both halves. From Payoff moments in `creator.md`; this is where the build log appears.

## Production notes

- Audio: voiceover recorded after the screen capture. Keep the real key and tap sounds in the mix, low.
  No music under the first three seconds.
- Shots: one continuous screen recording at 60fps, cut into five segments. The empty prompt box in
  segment one is a scratch project, so no client code and no private repo ever ships.
- Text style: burned-in labels, five words or fewer, anchored top-left so they never cover the file.
- Read time: about 42 seconds at 2.5 words per second, counting the primary hook and the primary CTA.
- Word count: 75 spoken words plus 20 on-screen words in the Beats table, 95 against a budget of 100.
  Bracketed markers count as zero.
- Beat five holds a placeholder. Without the real issue count, cut it and end on the split screen.

## What changed vs source

Kept: the bold claim mechanism, the uninterrupted screen recording, and the habit of naming what is about
to happen a beat before it happens. The source also opened on the blank state before showing its own.

Changed, roughly 20 percent: the source demoed a plugin, ours demos a file anyone could write in a text
editor, which is the only part of this a viewer can copy tonight. The source proved itself with a
follower screenshot, and we cannot use one, because no testimonial is listed under Proof assets, so beat
five carries a placeholder instead. All wording is ours.
