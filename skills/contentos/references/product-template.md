# Product template

This is the shape of `.contentos/product.md`. `setup` writes the short
answers a founder gives it under these headings and leaves the rest for the
founder to fill in by hand. Every other stage reads this file as ground
truth: a claim, a feature, or a word that is not written here does not exist
for the writer or the reviewer. Keep each answer short and concrete. Plain
language, no em dashes.

## Product
Name the product and say what it does, in words a stranger would understand
on the first read.

## One-liner
One sentence that explains the product to someone who has never heard of it.

## Audience profile
Describe the one person this is for, specific enough that a writer could
picture them walking into a room.

- Who specifically: a real kind of person, not a demographic slice.
- Their number one frustration, in their own words: how they would actually say it, not how a brand would.
- What they already tried: the other apps, habits, or workarounds that did not stick.
- The fear behind it: what they are actually afraid of if nothing changes.
- The outcome they want: the specific result, not a vague feeling.
- Top 3 objections: the reasons they would not act even if they wanted to.
- The proof that flips them: what fact or demo actually moves this person.

## Core features
List the 3 to 6 features a script is allowed to describe.

## Demo moments
Name the specific on-screen moments a script can show, the ones that make
the product's case without a word of narration.

## Allowed claims
List every claim, number, or comparison the writer may state as fact.

## Forbidden claims
List what this product must never claim: medical outcomes, income promises,
legal guarantees, or anything you are not ready to defend to a regulator.

## Proof assets
List the testimonials, reviews, screenshots, or user counts that genuinely
exist and may be shown on screen.

## Brand voice
Describe how this product sounds when it talks, specific enough that two
different writers would land on the same voice.

- 3 adjectives it is: the words that describe the voice at its best.
- 3 adjectives it is not: the nearest wrong turns, named so a writer avoids them.
- Sentence rules: how long a sentence runs, contractions or not, first or second person.
- 10 on-brand words: words this product's voice actually reaches for.
- 10 off-limits words: words that would make a founder wince if the writer used them.
- 3 sample sentences: real sentences in the voice, ready to imitate.

## CTA
Give the one action a viewer should take, and the objection that stops most
people from taking it.

## Hashtag seeds
List 10 to 20 hashtags the writer can mix into a caption: some broad, some
narrow to this exact product.

## Competitors
List the 3 to 8 Instagram accounts research scrapes for outliers, by handle.
